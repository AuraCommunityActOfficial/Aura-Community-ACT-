## Project publishing checklist
For each project, add:
- a one-sentence description and intended audience;
- current status: prototype, beta, released, paused, or archived;
- official website and source repository;
- screenshots or a short demo;
- supported platforms and requirements;
- privacy policy and support route where applicable;
- third-party credits and licenses.

Do not describe a project as released, verified, secure, or officially partnered unless that status can be substantiated.
