# Privacy Policy

**Aura Community ACT**  
**Effective date:** 27 November 2026  
**Website:** https://auracommunityact.github.io/Auracommunityact

## 1. About this policy
This policy explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when you visit Aura Community ACT websites, repositories, or project pages. The actual data collected depends on the services and features enabled on each site or product.

Aura Community ACT is an independent initiative. This policy is not a claim that every listed service or data practice is currently active.

## 2. Information you choose to provide
If you contact us through a published contact form, email address, GitHub issue, or other channel, you may provide information such as your name, username, contact details, and the contents of your message. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive information through public channels.

## 3. Technical information and hosting
The hosting provider and platforms used to deliver a website or repository may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply. For GitHub Pages, GitHub's policies apply to hosting-related processing.

## 4. Cookies, analytics, and embedded services
A site may use cookies or similar technologies if required by its hosting, embedded content, analytics, or other enabled features. This policy should be updated before adding analytics, advertising, account systems, or tracking technologies. Third-party embeds (for example, video players) may collect information under their own policies.

## 5. How information may be used
Information voluntarily sent to us may be used to:
- respond to questions and support requests;
- maintain, protect, and improve projects;
- investigate abuse, security issues, or service failures; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not state that we sell personal information. Do not add a claim about data sale, advertising, or analytics until the actual services and configuration have been checked.

## 6. Sharing
Information may be processed by service providers that host or operate the relevant website, repository, form, or communication service. We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the project, or the public.

## 7. Retention and security
Messages may be retained for as long as needed to respond, maintain records, address security concerns, or meet legal requirements. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Children
Our public pages are not intended to request sensitive personal information from children. Anyone under the age of 18 should seek a parent or guardian's help before sharing personal information online.

## 9. Your choices and requests
For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to Aura Community ACT, use the contact method published on the official website or GitHub organization. Information held by GitHub, a hosting provider, or another third party must generally be requested from that provider.

## 10. External links
Our pages may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information.

## 11. Changes
We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published.
