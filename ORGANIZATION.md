# Aura Community ACT

**Building Technology. Empowering Communities.**  
**Innovation · Technology · Community**

## Organization overview
Aura Community ACT is an independent, community-focused technology and software development initiative. It explores and builds digital products, software applications, educational tools, creative technology projects, and online community experiences.

Aura Community ACT is presented as an independent initiative. This profile does not claim that it is a registered company, a Google partner, or endorsed by any third party.

## Mission
To make useful technology more accessible by learning, building, documenting, and sharing practical digital solutions.

## Vision
To grow a responsible technology community that encourages creativity, technical learning, collaboration, and useful digital innovation.

## Areas of work
- **Software and app development:** experiments and products for mobile and web.
- **Educational technology:** learning resources and digital study experiences.
- **AI exploration:** responsible experimentation with AI-powered tools.
- **Creative technology:** design, games, media, and digital experiences.
- **Community technology:** tools and online spaces that help people connect and learn.

## Selected projects
Project descriptions and release status should be checked against each project's official page before publication.

- **Aura Learning** — an educational app initiative offering digital learning resources and study-focused features.
- **Aura Puzzles: Snap & Merge** — a mobile puzzle game project.
- **Aura Community ACT Bot** — a Discord community bot project.
- **Aura Music Studio** — a creative music and digital-audio initiative.
- **Aura AI** — an AI experimentation and assistant project.

## Working principles
- Build honestly; distinguish released products from prototypes and experiments.
- Respect user privacy, security, accessibility, and third-party licenses.
- Publish clear documentation and accurate project status.
- Welcome constructive feedback and responsible collaboration.
